# Focus Mode — Anti-Doomscroll (Firefox MV3)

A lightweight Firefox extension designed to block infinite-scroll and short-form video traps across Instagram, YouTube, and Twitter/X, helping you stay focused.

## Features

- **Instagram:** Block Reels & Explore, force the chronological "Following" feed, hide suggested/sponsored posts, hide the Stories tray, and block comments.
- **YouTube:** Block Shorts, hide recommendation sidebars and end-screen overlays, clean up comments, and optionally use a blank search-only homepage.
- **Twitter/X:** Default to the "Following" feed ("For You" suppression) and hide the Trending module.

---

## How to Load in Firefox

1. Open Firefox and navigate to `about:debugging`.
2. Click **This Firefox** in the left sidebar.
3. Click **Load Temporary Add-on…**.
4. Navigate into your `focus-mode-extension/` folder and select your **`manifest.json`** file.
5. Click the extension icon in your toolbar to customize your blocking toggles.

*(Note: Temporary add-ons reset when Firefox restarts.)*
